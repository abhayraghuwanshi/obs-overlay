Browser-native stream overlays

Design your stream overlay, then drop one link into OBS.

Overlay Studio is a free stream overlay editor that runs in your browser. Arrange cameras, live match scores, follower goals, timers, pets and themes on a real 16:9 canvas — then add the overlay to OBS as a browser source with a single link. Nothing to install.

What's inside

  • Drag-and-drop editor — place cameras, text and widgets on a live 16:9 canvas. No code.
  • OBS browser source — one link drops the overlay straight into OBS, and edits sync live.
  • Live match scoreboard — pick a fixture and the score keeps itself up to date on stream.
  • Pets & mood ring — little mascots that roam the canvas and shift with the mood of the room.
  • Goals, timers & widgets — follower and sub goals, countdowns, a pomodoro, tickers, sticky notes, a spin wheel.
  • Themes & recording — re-skin the whole overlay in a click, and record the stream right in the browser.
